# AstroHealth replaces the BASELINE MVP

The team chose the AstroHealth closed loop ([06-astrohealth-concept.md](../product/06-astrohealth-concept.md)) as the product. It replaces the MVP list in [04-directions.md](../product/04-directions.md) as the hackathon contract. We did not merge it into direction A. A future reader may ask why the hazard panel, the DONKI space-weather feed and the mission-profile switch are not in the MVP: this decision dropped them.
